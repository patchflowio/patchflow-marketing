01 / AUDIO PATCH

The foundation.
Mapped clearly.

Document every input from the source to the console. PatchFlow connects channel names, physical snake positions, and console assignments so the signal path stays understandable long after setup day.

  • Map console channels to physical snake and stage-box inputs
  • Organize drums, vocals, guitars, keys, and special inputs
  • Move a source and let the weekly change report explain it

02 / LIGHTING PLOT

A place for
every fixture.

Build a visual layout of your lighting rig. Place fixtures where they belong, keep their numbers and DMX addresses in view, and make the setup easier for the next person to understand.

  • Drag, align, and distribute fixtures on desktop
  • Group fixtures and zoom into the details
  • Export the plot as a PDF for your team

On mobile, tap fixtures for details. Arrange the plot on desktop.

03 / VIDEO CANVAS

See the signal.
End to end.

Put your sources, switchers, routers, and destinations on a connected canvas. Follow the path from camera to screen, with the ports and connections that make it work.

  • See physical connections and router crosspoints
  • Trace a signal path through the system
  • Keep device details and cable labels close

04 / NETWORK REFERENCE

Every address.
Easy to find.

Stop hunting for the right IP. Bring your networked production devices into one searchable reference, with the details you need when it’s time to connect or troubleshoot.

  • Search devices, IPs, hostnames, VLANs, and locations
  • Keep subnet, gateway, and assignment details together
  • Copy an IP or open a device’s web interface
